Team

Meet the people behind the sound.

FGC Studio is led by creatives who understand both the business and the studio side of making music.

CEO / Owner Operator

Moka Blast

Moka is a recording artist and entrepreneur who founded FGC Studio to give artists a professional space to create and develop their music. Born in Trinidad & Tobago and raised in New York, he built his career through independent music, touring, and creative business ventures before establishing the studio in Las Vegas.

Co-Owner & Chief Producer / Engineer

Nick “MEEK”

Nick “MEEK” is a producer, recording engineer, and mixing/mastering engineer known for creative energy and technical precision. With experience across Pop, Hip Hop, EDM, Punk Rock, Latin, Country, and film music, he helps artists turn ideas into fully realized records.
